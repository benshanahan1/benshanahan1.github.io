## Work experience

### 2026 - Present: Co-Founder, AI & Robotics
**Senra Systems, Cypress, CA**

Lead Senra's robotics program, applying AI and robotics to high-mix wire harness assembly:
vision for inspection, robots for the hardest assembly steps, and agents across our software.
Building the team across production robotics, robot learning, and perception.

### 2023 - 2026: Co-Founder, CTO
**Senra Systems, Torrance, CA** ([senrasystems.us](https://www.senrasystems.us))

Co-founded Senra with Jordan Black to rethink how wire harnesses are designed and built. Built
Amp, the platform that runs our factory: engineers turn customer drawings into work
instructions, technicians build from digital instructions at their stations, and every harness
ends up with a full build and inspection record.

2022 CV description: Senra Systems supercharges manufacturing through software configuration,
transforming skilled assembly tasks into high-throughput production lines. We enable customers
to design what they want, when they want it and deliver builds faster than any other company
on the planet.

### 2022 - 2023: Software Engineer II
**SpaceX, Hawthorne, CA**

Worked on many systems: a distributed telemetry system used by Falcon and Dragon launches, an
Enterprise Resource Planning software powering the entire company (Warpdrive, SpaceX's in-house
ERP and execution system), a distributed microservice-based Manufacturing Execution System used
by Starlink for high-rate antenna dish and wireless router manufacturing, and a telemetry system
responsible for ingesting and processing high-rate real-time data from rockets, engines,
satellites, and more.

Wore many hats: software engineer, product manager, project manager. Understood manufacturing
processes in the factory, met with stakeholders, scoped requirements, set and communicated
timelines, designed, implemented, and supported software including Falcon 9, Starlink, and
Starship. Improved team processes, documentation, and ability to share knowledge. Helped onboard
and train new engineers.

Manager: Kristine Huang

### 2020 - 2022: Software Engineer
**SpaceX, Hawthorne, CA**

Joined the Warpdrive team. From mid-2021 to mid-2022, on the Manufacturing Execution System team
in the Starlink factory, building user terminals and Wi-Fi routers at rate.

Managers (by recency): Tom Kiernan, Eric Lloyd, Anthony Rose, Steve Sepanloo

### 2018 - 2021: Research Software Engineer
**NeuroPace, Mountain View, CA**

Full-stack design and development of internal web-based tools for research and field teams.
Focus was on interfaces for analysis and interactive visualization of large neural data sets,
collected by an implanted neurostimulator to help patients with epilepsy. Linux server and
Oracle database administration and monitoring. Left in 2020 and kept contracting for another six
months, into 2021.

Manager: Tom Tcheng, Ph.D.

### 2017 - 2018: Computer Programmer
**BrainGate, Brown University, Providence, RI**

- Reverse-engineered and re-implemented the software interface to the neural recording software
