\begin{newSection}{Work Experience}

\fourNoIta{2026 - Present}
{Co-Founder, AI \& Robotics}
{Senra Systems, Cypress, CA}

\fourNoIta{2023 - 2026}
{Co-Founder, CTO}
{Senra Systems, Torrance, CA}
{Co-founded Senra with Jordan Black to rethink how wire harnesses are designed and built. Built Amp, the platform that runs our factory: engineers turn customer drawings into work instructions, technicians build from digital instructions at their stations, and every harness ends up with a full build and inspection record. \href{https://www.senrasystems.us}{senrasystems.us}}

\fourNoIta{2022 - 2023}
{Software Engineer II}
{SpaceX, Hawthorne, CA}
{Telemetry system that ingests high-rate continuous data from engine tests, launches, and satellites, plus Warpdrive, SpaceX's in-house ERP and execution system. Worked across engineering, product, and project management: met with factory stakeholders, scoped requirements, set timelines, and onboarded new engineers on software supporting Falcon 9, Starlink, and Starship.}

\fourNoIta{2020 - 2022}
{Software Engineer}
{SpaceX, Hawthorne, CA}
{Joined the Warpdrive team. From mid-2021 to mid-2022, on the Manufacturing Execution System team in the Starlink factory, building user terminals and Wi-Fi routers at rate.}

\fourNoIta{2018 - 2021}
{Research Software Engineer}
{NeuroPace, Mountain View, CA}
{Full-stack design and development of internal tools for research and field teams, focused on analysis and interactive visualization of large neural data sets from an implanted neurostimulator that treats epilepsy. Linux server and Oracle database administration.}

\fourNoIta{2017 - 2018}
{Computer Programmer}
{BrainGate, Brown University, Providence, RI}
{Co-led the C implementation of the BrainGate calibration and decoder pipeline on an embedded Linux system, and reverse-engineered the interface to the neural recording software. Debugged FPGA signal processing code in Vivado HLS. Led the real-time monitoring system for the BrainGate research cart (Python, websockets, MongoDB). Supervised a high school intern building a calibration game. \emph{Supervisors: Dr. John Simeral \& Dr. Leigh Hochberg}}

\fourNoIta{2016 - 2017}
{Research Assistant}
{BrainGate, Brown University, Providence, RI}
{Implemented steady state Kalman filter in closed-loop auto-updating brain-computer interface (BCI) calibration system. Honors thesis research in idle state detection in BCIs in human motor and premotor cortex. \emph{Supervisors: Dr. Leigh Hochberg \& Dr. David Brandman}}
	
\fourNoIta{2015 - 2016}
{Research Assistant}
{Omarlab, Brown University, Providence, RI}
{Analyzed shape of brain rhythms in running rats to evaluate their usefulness as an analytical metric. Work on figures and logic for first-author journal article. Developed Matlab toolbox for brain rhythm shape analysis. Shadowed epileptologist at Rhode Island Hospital. \emph{Supervisor: Dr. Omar Ahmed}}

\fourNoIta{2014 - 2017}
{Matlab Consultant}
{Burwell Lab, Brown University, Providence, RI}
{Wrote and maintained Matlab code for colleagues' analyses and data processing, and tutored lab members in Matlab. \emph{Supervisor: Dr. Rebecca Burwell}}

\fourNoIta{Summer 2014, 2013}
{Research Assistant}
{Cashlab, Massachusetts General Hosp., Boston, MA}
{Developed affordable OpBox rat operant conditioning chamber with Dr. Eyal Kimchi. Developed Semiology Diagnostic Tool to objectively diagnose region of seizure onset and settle conflicts during surgical meetings. Developed Matlab toolbox to detect high-frequency oscillations in epilepsy data. \emph{Supervisors: Dr. Sydney Cash \& Dr. Brandon Westover}}
